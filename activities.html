Leadership & Service

Contributing Beyond the Classroom

My engagement at Mahindra University extends beyond my role in the classroom. I have taken on responsibilities that involve student life, institutional initiatives and the development of opportunities for students to learn through experience.

These experiences have allowed me to work closely with students in settings where leadership, discipline, responsibility and collaboration matter as much as academic knowledge.
