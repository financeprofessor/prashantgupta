Research

My research examines corporate governance, banking and asset quality, Central Bank Digital Currencies, financial inclusion, FinTech, digital payments, and emerging applications of technology in finance.

Research Agenda

Three major areas of research

My research agenda combines established work in banking and corporate governance with emerging research on Central Bank Digital Currencies, FinTech, financial inclusion, digital payments, and technology-enabled financial services. Each research area below brings together the related publications, ongoing studies, and conference presentations.
