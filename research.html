Core Research Areas

My research agenda focuses on understanding how governance, institutional structures, and emerging financial technologies shape financial outcomes.

My published and ongoing research spans corporate governance, bank asset quality, and the rapidly evolving literature on Central Bank Digital Currencies.

Research Areas

Areas of Inquiry

Three interconnected areas form the core of my current research agenda.

Corporate Governance

Research examining the relationship between corporate governance, legal frameworks, institutional characteristics, and financial outcomes.

Banking & Asset Quality

Research focused on bank asset quality, non-performing assets, corporate governance, and differences between government-owned and private banks.

Central Bank Digital Currencies

Research exploring the development of CBDC scholarship and its implications for financial systems, institutions, and economies.
