Research Perspective

From Financial Institutions to Emerging Digital Systems

My research journey has evolved from questions around corporate governance and banking asset quality toward emerging questions in digital finance. Across these areas, I remain interested in how institutions, policies, technologies and financial systems interact and shape outcomes in emerging economies.
