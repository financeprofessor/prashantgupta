Research

My research focuses on understanding financial institutions, governance mechanisms, banking stability, and emerging digital financial systems.

Corporate Governance

My research examines the role of corporate governance mechanisms in shaping the performance and risk outcomes of financial institutions, with particular attention to governance structures and their implications for banking.

My published research in this area includes studies examining corporate governance determinants of bank asset quality and the role of governance in explaining differences between government-owned and private banks.

Banking & Asset Quality

A major area of my research is banking, with a particular focus on asset quality and non-performing assets in the banking sector.

My work examines factors associated with bank asset quality, including corporate governance, regulatory and institutional factors, and capital adequacy components.

Central Bank Digital Currencies (CBDCs)

My recent research examines the rapidly developing literature on Central Bank Digital Currencies (CBDCs), with an emphasis on understanding the evolving research landscape and its implications for financial systems.

Current work includes systematic and structured reviews of CBDC research across economies and income groups, examining themes related to design, adoption, financial stability, banking, regulation, and the broader digital transformation of finance.
