Teaching

Teaching Finance. Building Understanding.

I approach finance and accounting as subjects to be understood, practised and applied—not simply memorised.

Teaching Philosophy

From Concepts to Application

My teaching focuses on helping students develop a clear conceptual understanding of finance and accounting while connecting classroom concepts with practical business situations.

I place particular emphasis on practice, structured problem solving and the ability to interpret financial information rather than simply arriving at an answer.

“The objective is not just to teach students a formula, but to help them understand when, why and how to use it.”

Classroom Approach

How I Teach

My classroom approach is designed to move students from understanding concepts to applying them independently.

Concept First

Concepts are introduced with emphasis on clarity, logic and understanding before moving into application.

Practice-Based Learning

Students learn by working through problems, exercises and practical financial analysis.

Real-World Context

Classroom discussions are connected to businesses, financial statements and situations encountered in practice.
