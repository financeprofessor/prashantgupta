Teaching Philosophy

From concepts to application

I believe finance and accounting are best understood when students can connect classroom concepts with actual business situations. My teaching therefore focuses on building strong fundamentals while encouraging students to analyse information, solve problems, work with financial data, and understand the implications of financial decisions.

How I Teach

A practice-oriented approach

The classroom is designed to move beyond simply learning formulas or definitions. The emphasis is on understanding, application, and independent thinking.

Conceptual Clarity

Strong fundamentals help students understand not only what a financial concept is, but also why it matters and when it should be applied.

Real-World Context

Classroom discussions are connected with businesses, financial statements, markets, and situations that students may encounter in professional life.

Learning by Doing

Students are encouraged to analyse information, work through problems, and apply financial concepts rather than relying only on passive learning.
