Teaching & Academic Experience

Teaching Finance.
Building Understanding.

My teaching focuses on making finance and accounting concepts practical, structured and relevant to real-world business decisions.

Teaching Philosophy

Learning by Understanding, Applying and Doing

I believe that finance and accounting are best understood when students can connect classroom concepts with actual business situations.

My teaching therefore combines conceptual clarity with practical application, real-world examples, financial analysis, Excel-based exercises and case-based learning.

The objective is not simply to help students learn formulas, but to develop their ability to interpret financial information and use it to make informed managerial decisions.

“Finance becomes meaningful when students can see how concepts work beyond the classroom.”
