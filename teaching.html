Teaching Philosophy

From Concepts to Application

The objective is not simply to explain financial concepts, but to help students understand how those concepts work in real organisations and real decisions.

Conceptual Clarity Build a strong understanding of the underlying financial concepts.
Real-World Examples Connect classroom concepts with actual businesses, financial decisions and contemporary situations.
Practice-Based Learning Use exercises, cases, Excel-based applications and problem solving to move from theory to practice.
Student Engagement Encourage questions, discussion and active participation in the learning process.
