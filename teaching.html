Teaching Philosophy

From concepts to application.

Finance is best understood when students can connect classroom concepts with the decisions faced by businesses, managers and financial institutions.

My teaching approach combines conceptual clarity, practical application and active learning, with an emphasis on helping students understand not only what a financial concept is, but also why it matters and how it is applied.

“The objective is not simply to teach finance, but to help students think financially.”

How I Teach

Three principles guide my classroom.

Conceptual Clarity

Building a strong understanding of financial concepts before moving to their application.

Real-World Context

Connecting classroom learning with companies, markets, financial institutions and contemporary business situations.

Learning by Doing

Using practical exercises, financial models, analysis and decision-making activities to reinforce classroom concepts.
