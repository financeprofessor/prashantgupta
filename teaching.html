Teaching

My teaching focuses on connecting accounting and finance concepts with practical business applications and decision-making.

Teaching Philosophy

I believe finance and accounting are best understood when students can connect theoretical concepts with real-world business situations. My teaching therefore emphasizes practical examples, problem-solving, financial analysis, and application of concepts to business decisions.

I also encourage students to develop analytical thinking and confidence in working with financial information rather than relying only on theoretical understanding.

Practice-Based Learning

My approach to teaching emphasizes hands-on learning and the use of real-world business examples. Students are encouraged to apply financial concepts through practical exercises, financial analysis, and spreadsheet-based modelling.

Student Development

Beyond classroom teaching, I have been involved in student mentoring, admissions interviews, outreach activities, and academic and institutional initiatives aimed at supporting student development.
