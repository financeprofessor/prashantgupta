A Short Profile

Dr. Prashant Gupta is an Assistant Professor at the School of Management, Mahindra University, Hyderabad, with academic interests spanning corporate governance, banking and asset quality, and Central Bank Digital Currencies.

His academic journey combines doctoral research in Finance at IIT Delhi with teaching experience across management and higher-education institutions.

Beyond teaching and research, he has been involved in institutional development, academic quality initiatives, student development and leadership responsibilities.
