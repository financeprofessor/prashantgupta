My Story

A Journey in Academia

My academic journey began with a B.Com. in Accounts Honours from St. Xavier's College, Ranchi, followed by an MBA in Finance from BIT Mesra. I subsequently pursued a Ph.D. in Finance at IIT Delhi, where my doctoral research focused on corporate governance and asset quality in the banking sector.

Over the years, I have worked across teaching, research and academic administration. My professional experience spans institutions including PIBM, PSIT, IIT Delhi, the Indian School of Business and NALSAR University of Law.

Since July 2022, I have been an Assistant Professor at the School of Management, Mahindra University, Hyderabad. Alongside teaching and research, I contribute to academic quality, institutional development and student initiatives.

My interests extend beyond conventional academic work. Through NCC and my long-standing association with Art of Living, I remain engaged in leadership, youth development and personal excellence.

Beyond Academia

Learning, Leadership & Service

My professional journey has reinforced my interest in connecting academic learning with practical experience, institutional responsibility and student development.

In addition to my academic responsibilities, I serve in institutional leadership roles and work with NCC cadets. I have also been associated with Art of Living since 2011, facilitating programmes focused on personal development and youth empowerment.

These experiences form an important part of my journey, complementing my work as a teacher and researcher.
