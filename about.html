Profile

About Dr. Prashant Gupta

I am an Assistant Professor at the School of Management, Mahindra University, Hyderabad, with a Ph.D. in Finance from IIT Delhi.

My research interests include corporate governance, banking and asset quality, Central Bank Digital Currencies and emerging areas of digital finance.

My academic interests are closely connected with my teaching, where I focus on making finance and accounting concepts practical, structured and relevant to real-world business situations.

Research. Teaching. Development.

My academic work combines research in finance with classroom teaching and sustained engagement in student development and institutional activities.
