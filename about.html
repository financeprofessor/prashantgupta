Academic & Professional Profile

Academic Journey

Dr. Prashant Gupta is an Assistant Professor at the School of Management, Mahindra University, Hyderabad.

His academic background is rooted in finance, with a Ph.D. in Finance from IIT Delhi, an MBA in Finance from BIT Mesra and a B.Com. in Accounts Honours from St. Xavier's College, Ranchi.

His professional journey includes academic and teaching roles at institutions including Mahindra University, NALSAR University of Law, PSIT and PIBM, along with academic experience at IIT Delhi and the Indian School of Business.
