From Finance to Academic Leadership

I am Dr. Prashant Gupta, an Assistant Professor at the School of Management, Mahindra University, Hyderabad.

My academic journey has been shaped by an interest in finance, corporate governance, banking and emerging areas of digital finance. My research examines questions at the intersection of corporate governance, banking and asset quality, while also extending into Central Bank Digital Currencies, financial inclusion and FinTech.

Alongside teaching and research, I have remained actively involved in institutional responsibilities, student development, leadership initiatives and professional engagement.
