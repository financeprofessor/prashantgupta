A Short Profile

An Academic Journey Built Around Learning

I am an Assistant Professor at the School of Management, Mahindra University, Hyderabad. My academic career has brought together teaching, research, institutional engagement and opportunities to work closely with students.

Over the years, I have had the opportunity to work across universities, business schools and educational institutions, experiencing different dimensions of academic life and management education.

I value an academic environment where knowledge is connected with practice, ideas are explored with curiosity, and students are encouraged to grow both professionally and personally.

Teaching, learning and contributing to the academic community remain at the centre of my professional journey.
