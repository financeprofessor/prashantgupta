Assistant Professor of Accounting and Finance at the School of Management, Mahindra University, Hyderabad, with interests spanning teaching, research, academic leadership, and student development.

Professional Profile

Accounting & Finance · Research · Teaching · Academic Leadership

Dr. Prashant Gupta is an Assistant Professor of Accounting and Finance at the School of Management, Mahindra University, Hyderabad. He holds a Ph.D. in Finance from IIT Delhi and has over a decade of experience spanning teaching, academic administration, research, and student development.

His academic work combines teaching and research in finance and accounting, with particular emphasis on corporate governance, banking and asset quality, and Central Bank Digital Currencies.

Beyond the classroom, he contributes to institutional responsibilities, student development, academic quality initiatives, and well-being programmes.

Research

Areas of Inquiry

His research focuses on questions related to governance, banking, asset quality, and emerging forms of digital money.

Corporate Governance

Research examining governance structures, legal frameworks, and institutional characteristics and their relationship with corporate and banking outcomes.

Banking & Asset Quality

Research focused on bank asset quality, non-performing assets, corporate governance, and differences across banking institutions.

Central Bank Digital Currencies

Research exploring the evolving CBDC literature and its implications for financial systems and economies.
