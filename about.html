About Dr. Prashant Gupta

Assistant Professor of Accounting and Finance at the School of Management, Mahindra University, Hyderabad.

Professional Profile

Dr. Prashant Gupta is an Assistant Professor of Accounting and Finance at the School of Management, Mahindra University, Hyderabad. He holds a Ph.D. in Finance from IIT Delhi and has over a decade of experience spanning teaching, academic administration, research, and student development.

His academic work combines teaching and research in finance and accounting, with particular emphasis on corporate governance, banking and asset quality, and Central Bank Digital Currencies (CBDCs).

Research

His research focuses primarily on corporate governance, bank asset quality, and Central Bank Digital Currencies. His research has been published in international academic journals, and he has presented research at national and international conferences.

He has also received Best Paper Awards and has been involved in research projects supported by Government of India ministries.

Academic Experience

Alongside teaching and research, Dr. Gupta has contributed to academic governance, accreditation initiatives, student development, and institutional administration.

His previous academic and professional experience includes roles associated with IIT Delhi, NALSAR University of Law, the Indian School of Business, PSIT Kanpur, and PIBM Pune.

Teaching

His teaching experience includes Financial Accounting, Management Accounting, Financial Management, Advanced Financial Management, Cost & Management Accounting, and Enterprise Risk Management.

His teaching approach emphasizes practical understanding, real-world applications, and connecting financial concepts with business decision-making.

Leadership & Well-being

Dr. Gupta is also associated with the Art of Living and has been involved in leadership and well-being programmes for students and youth across India. He has also contributed to stress-management and rehabilitation programmes at Tihar and Hotwar Central Jails.
