Research

My research focuses on contemporary issues in finance and financial institutions, with particular emphasis on corporate governance, banking and asset quality, and Central Bank Digital Currencies (CBDCs).



Corporate Governance

Research examining corporate governance mechanisms and their implications for financial-sector outcomes.

Banking & Asset Quality

Research focusing on bank asset quality, non-performing assets, governance, and related issues in the banking sector.

Central Bank Digital Currencies

Research examining the evolving CBDC landscape, including CBDC development, systematic reviews, and differences across economies.
