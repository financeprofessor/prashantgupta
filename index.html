Welcome

Teaching, Research & Student Development

I am an Assistant Professor at the School of Management, Mahindra University, Hyderabad, with a Ph.D. in Finance from IIT Delhi.

My academic work focuses on corporate governance, banking and asset quality, while my recent research also explores Central Bank Digital Currencies, FinTech and digital finance.

Alongside research and teaching, I remain actively involved in student development and institutional activities.
