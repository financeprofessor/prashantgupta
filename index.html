Teaching

Learning by doing.

My teaching approach combines conceptual clarity with practical application. I use real-world examples, business situations and hands-on exercises to help students connect financial concepts with managerial decision-making.

I particularly emphasize analytical thinking, spreadsheet-based applications and the ability to interpret financial information in practical settings.
