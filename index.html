Research

Areas of Inquiry

My research explores questions at the intersection of governance, banking, financial stability, and emerging digital forms of money.

Corporate Governance

Research examining how governance structures, legal frameworks, and institutional characteristics influence corporate and banking outcomes.

Banking & Asset Quality

Research focused on bank asset quality, non-performing assets, corporate governance, and differences across banking institutions.

Central Bank Digital Currencies

Research exploring the evolving academic landscape surrounding Central Bank Digital Currencies and their implications for financial systems and economies.

Teaching

Practice-oriented learning

My teaching approach emphasizes conceptual clarity, practical application, and connecting financial concepts with real-world business decision-making.

I use practical examples, analytical exercises, and hands-on learning to help students develop confidence in applying accounting and finance concepts.
