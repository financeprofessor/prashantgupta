Dr. Prashant Gupta

Assistant Professor – Accounting and Finance

School of Management, Mahindra University, Hyderabad

I am an academic and researcher working at the intersection of accounting, finance, banking and emerging digital financial systems. My research interests include corporate governance, banking and asset quality, and Central Bank Digital Currencies (CBDCs).

About Me

Research

My research focuses on understanding important issues in financial institutions, corporate governance and emerging digital financial technologies.

Corporate Governance

Research examining corporate governance mechanisms and their implications for organisational and financial outcomes.

Banking & Asset Quality

Research on bank asset quality, non-performing assets and the role of governance and institutional factors in banking.

Central Bank Digital Currencies

Research exploring the evolving literature, design, adoption and broader implications of Central Bank Digital Currencies.
