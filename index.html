About Me

I am an Assistant Professor of Finance at the School of Management, Mahindra University, Hyderabad. I hold a PhD in Management from IIT Delhi, with a research focus on finance, corporate governance, and banking.

My broader academic interests include corporate finance, banking, financial technology, artificial intelligence in finance, Central Bank Digital Currencies (CBDCs), and financial markets.

In addition to teaching and research, I contribute to academic administration, student development, accreditation initiatives, and institutional activities.

Research

My research examines contemporary issues at the intersection of finance, technology, governance, and banking.

Corporate Governance

Corporate governance, firm-level decision making, and financial performance.

Banking & Finance

Banking sector performance, asset quality, financial stability, and related issues.

AI in Finance

Applications and implications of artificial intelligence in financial decision making.

Central Bank Digital Currencies

CBDC design, adoption, financial inclusion, financial stability, and implications for financial systems.

Financial Technology

Emerging technologies and their impact on financial institutions and markets.

Financial Markets

Research examining financial markets, institutions, and emerging developments in finance.
